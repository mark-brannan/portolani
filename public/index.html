        Charts

        A chart's picture or name flies to it and lays its scan over the map as one sheet, alone and at its own placement, to drag, turn and resize by hand; the eye shows or hides that one sheet and leaves the others and the map as they are; its city flies there.

            About

            Ornaments drawn as vectors after, or cropped from, public-domain scans via Wikimedia Commons: Olaus Magnus, Carta Marina (1539); Jorge de Aguiar, wind rose and chart (1492, Beinecke Library, Yale); Cresques Abraham, Catalan Atlas (1375, BnF Gallica); a Genoese chart (c. 1320-1350, Library of Congress); Frederik de Wit, cartouche (Royal Museums Greenwich). Coasts and places: Natural Earth. Lettering: IM Fell English (SIL OFL).
