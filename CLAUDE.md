# Portolani

- **A new visual decoration ships on by default**: a sprite kind, a style, a drawn feature. Put it in the page's `DEFAULTS` and in `defaultSprites`, unless the user asks otherwise, for example by raising it as a concern on the PR.
- **Headless scene options** (`relief`, `trees`, `rivers`, `lakes`) stay off by default, so each golden isolates one thing.
- **No dropdowns** in the settings drawer where icons, chips or checkboxes will do.
- **Option lists are chips**, not dropdowns or plain lists, and each chip shows what it does.
- **Every control shows its effect** where it can (a swatch, a line sample or a small example), not only a label.
- **Every PR body carries a way to demo it**: a live URL, or one command the user can run.
- **A scanned sprite is a soft-edged patch of the scan, not its ink alone.** The patch is the whole crop, feathered at its edge, so the sea a ship sits in, the swell and the ripples round a monster, the grass under a tree and the hatching round a village all come with the figure. An outline drawn inside the crop (a polygon, an ellipse, a wide silhouette) cuts out only what is not the subject: a neighbouring figure, lettering, a border, a fold. It never trims the subject's surroundings, and its feather sits outside the ink, never across a tail tip or a yard. Round a monster the water is the big one: take all of it and every scale of the beast rather than trim a little water and lose any of the monster. A ship or a monster is an object in the water, so its crop box reaches well below the figure and the bottom edge keeps the most water: the water drawn over the hull or the belly is what sells the illusion that it sits in the sea, so a box that crops close under the figure is the first thing to widen. Divide the paper out and let the renderer's multiply blend sink the patch into the parchment. Never key by darkness levels on a coloured plate: it thins every wash and loses the surroundings. Levels key a pack drawn in ink alone, where nothing but line is wanted.
- **The raw crop is the bar.** The user would rather see the raw crop on the map than a filtered sprite. A sprite PR shows raw beside the sprite at map size, multiplied onto parchment, one row per sprite (a `steps.png` under `docs/<scan>/`), and anything in the raw that the sprite lacks is named in the README's row with its reason; a loss with no reason means the outline is too close, so widen it or drop it. A plain composite on parchment is not the after; the multiply is. `docs/sprite-patch/debry-monster.jpg` shows the de Bry monster raw, outlined round the figure (the swell and the sea gone), and as the whole crop (both kept).
