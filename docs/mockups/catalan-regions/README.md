# Mockups: the Catalan Atlas's own region names

Four ways to letter the map with the atlas's realms (Sarra, Tauris, Medeja, Delli, Organa, Nubia and the kingdoms of Europe), each at the Old World, zoom 3.4, with BnF sheet 9 beside it. Candidates 1 to 4 are drawn over the live scene and are not wired into the page; candidate 5 is live in the page.

1. `candidate-1.png`: the atlas's spelling, italic ink, as today's region names are lettered.
2. `candidate-2.png`: rubrics, spaced capitals in the atlas's alternating red and blue, realms larger than kingdoms (sheet 9's REY DEL TAURIS and ARABIA SEBBA are lettered so).
3. `candidate-3.png`: the spelling with a small English reading beneath it.
4. `candidate-4.png`: no lettering; a small enthroned king marks each realm, as the atlas draws its rulers, and a click opens the legend tile (Tauris shown open).
5. `candidate-5.png`, `candidate-5-popover.png`, `candidate-5-popover-placeholder.png`: the combination, live in the page (Regions, "1375 · Catalan Atlas", lettering "Red and blue, with readings"; `regionstyle=plain` in the link turns it off). The atlas's italic from 1, in the alternating red and blue of 2, a realm at 26 px and a kingdom at 21 px (between 1's 22 px and the 30 px capitals of 2) so the names stay clear of coasts and of each other, a small English reading under each as in 3, and a click on any name opens a tile with the atlas's legend and our note (every region has one; a placeholder stands where the source is thin). Ruler figures as scan sprites are deferred.
