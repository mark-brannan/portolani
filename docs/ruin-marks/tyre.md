# Tyre: where the two ruin marks stand

Tyre's tile (`scripts/sights.mjs`) draws a ruin at two spots, because the dug remains lie on two sites 1.7 km apart, both on UNESCO's list as one property (no. 299). The list button for Tyre still flies to a view centred between them.

| Spot | Lon, lat | What stands there | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| The city site (Al-Mina), on the old island's southern shore | 35.1933, 33.2681 (33°16′05″ N, 35°11′36″ E) | the colonnaded street, the agora, the public baths, the arena and the quays of the southern ("Egyptian") harbour, some columns now under water | [Tyre, Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyre,_Lebanon) (the city's own point is 33.27083, 35.19611, 400 m north-east, in the modern town) |
| Al-Bass, on the mainland by the tombolo | 35.2111, 33.2692 (33°16′09″ N, 35°12′40″ E) | the Roman and Byzantine necropolis along the road, the triumphal arch, the aqueduct, the hippodrome, and under them the Iron Age Phoenician cemetery dug since 1997 | [Tyre Necropolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyre_Necropolis) (its own point is 33.27222, 35.20972, 360 m north, inside the same site) |

Reverse-geocoded with OpenStreetMap's Nominatim: the second point answers "البص, صور" (Al-Bass, Tyre); the first answers "صور" (Tyre) and nothing finer.

Not Tyre: [Umm al-Amad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umm_al-Amad,_Lebanon) is a Hellenistic temple site near Naqoura at 33.128, 35.1535, 16 km south of the city site, and belongs to no mark here.
