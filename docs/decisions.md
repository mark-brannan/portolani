# Decisions

One line per ruling, newest last. An ADR supersedes a line here.

- 2026-10-13 — "The essential ingredient of drawing line-based maps is best suited to geojson data or similar vector based or vector-like representations of the maps that do not use tiles. Use coastlines or coast-wright in their new/reimagined versions. Or use natural earth geojson data. I want rich overlays for decorative elements if we can get that." Ruled by Solace. ([#26](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/issues/26))
- 2026-10-13 — "you should use coast-wright though.  It draws things!   If we don't have what we need inside coast-wright already then we can request it (change it ourselves) easily" Ruled by Solace. ([#26](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/issues/26))
- 2026-10-14 — Vectors, not raster; delivery is open: "Geometry is vector at every zoom and drawn through coast-wright. Whether it arrives as one file or as chunks by region and zoom band is an implementation matter. Raster is never the coastline." Clarifies the first 2026-10-13 line: "do not use tiles" means no raster tiles. Ruled by Solace. ([#26](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/issues/26))
- 2026-10-14 — The cartouche and the scale bar are on the window, not on the map: "pinned to the screen." Ruled by Solace. ([#107](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/issues/107), [docs/atlas](atlas/README.md))
- 2026-10-14 — Frame cost of ornaments projected while the map moves: "50ms is fabulous for something so heavy in terms of data...I think that's a high bar.  a few hundred ms is ok for now in the development phase." Ruled by Solace. ([#107](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/issues/107))
- 2026-10-14 — Chart traditions in the atlas: "to a degree portolani will have an inherent cultural bias that we already accept.  That itself is not a reason for concern." Open to curating chart sources from other traditions (the Arabic-speaking world, the Far East) for cross-cultural and historical interest. Ruled by Solace. ([#107](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/issues/107))
- 2026-10-14 — Scanned sprites keep their surroundings: "in all cases (or nearly all) I would rather see the raw image on the portolani map than the one we scanned and filtered"; "we might get better results for some of these scans if we intentionally kept part of the visual elements from the original parchment and surrounding background and just let it bleed through or blend with the canvas that we draw onto in our rendering"; "This looks like a general problem that we need to correct, not just a one-off for this image." On #112: "Waves, water, all around monsters is a really big one.  I would rather have all of the water and keep all of the monster detail, than trim a little water and loose any monster details."; "for ships or monsters where we are representing an object that is immersed: the more water (and the more bound box) we retain along the bottom edge, the easier it is to sell the illusion that the large object is truly *IN* the water." Ruled by Solace. ([#104](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/pull/104), [#111](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/issues/111), [#112](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/pull/112#discussion_r4277791146))
- 2026-10-15 — The shared link carries the reader's position: "By design, I want the links to include location for easy sharing (deterministic).  A city block is acceptable levels of privacy for this tool.  The real privacy guard is whether they allow us to gather location at all (restricted in most browsers) and then whether and whom they choose to share with." Ruled by Solace. ([#121](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/pull/121))
- 2026-10-15 — Overlapping curated sheets: the smaller sheet's ground is territory for every ornament, not only its lines. Solace: "It's strange to think that we interpretted the git hub issues as 'take all the decorations from all sheets and overlap them'". Ruled by Solace. ([#143](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/pull/143), [docs/atlas](atlas/README.md))
- 2026-10-15 — What ornaments are: "Generally I want the ornaments to be what they are: ornamentation, decoration, extra and non-functional but aesthetically essential." Ruled by Solace. ([#143](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/pull/143), [docs/atlas](atlas/README.md))
- 2026-10-15 — Roses and decoration may stand on land or sea, tentatively; size is bounded: "I am tentatively ok with using either land or see for roses and decoration, however we shouldn't just make massive roses that occupy all possibly space....that's not right either. It's still supposed to fundamentally be a map, a chart...not merely a canvas for art." Ruled by Solace. ([#143](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/pull/143), [docs/atlas](atlas/README.md))
- 2026-10-15 — Density is open to change: "I am ok with any changes to density now that we want to make." Ruled by Solace. ([#143](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/pull/143), [docs/atlas](atlas/README.md))
- 2026-10-15 — Historical accuracy mode is on by default: a view near a curated chart's own window shows that chart's own ornaments unless the reader turns the chip off. Ruled by Solace. ([#146](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/issues/146))
- 2026-10-20 — Voyage overlays: Columbus, da Gama, Dias, Magellan–Elcano, Drake, Zheng He, Leif Erikson, Marco Polo (overland included) and Ibn Battuta, on the modern-geometry map in every projection, never on the historic sheets. Confidence by stroke (solid attested, dashed inferred, dotted conventional); where the course is unknown, "we can just use a 'broad brush' and draw a smooth curve along either a great circle or some rhumb lines or the most likely paths": historical interest and education before precision. Ruled by Solace. ([docs/voyages](voyages/README.md), [#161](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/issues/161))
- 2026-10-20 — The voyages' menu: none shown by default; each voyage an item of a sub-menu under the gold dividers icon (PR #202), which is the voyages' own icon: Go keeps an icon of its own. A skull and crossbones where each voyage's named people died, on the track or off it, shown with that voyage. Ruled by Solace. ([docs/voyages](voyages/README.md))
- 2026-10-20 — More than one voyage at a time, in place of one at a time: "No, I don't like it this way 'one at a time'. [...] I want us to be able to select more than one at a time." Ruled by Solace. ([#208](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/pull/208#discussion_r4331707913), [docs/voyages](voyages/README.md))
- 2026-10-20 — Clear or reset every selection group, voyages and the rest: "I want us to be able to clear/reset all of them (I also want that as a general rule, for things like sea monsters, decorations, etc.)." Ruled by Solace. ([#208](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/pull/208#discussion_r4331707913))
- 2026-10-20 — A colour picker for voyage markings: "Critically, I want the voyage markings to be available with a color picker but maybe randomized colors when selecting multiple. The first one in red is fine but subsequent ones we might want to colorize differently." Ruled by Solace; the colours after the first are current thinking. ([#208](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/pull/208#discussion_r4331707913), [docs/voyages](voyages/README.md))
- 2026-10-20 — More voyages, from ancient history: "I probably also want to add some more voyages, possibly more than one for Columbus and Zheng He, but certainly for some voyages in ancient history also." Ruled by Solace for ancient voyages; more than one item for Columbus and Zheng He is current thinking, and reopens the earlier one item each (1492–93; the seventh fleet). ([#208](https://github.com/mark-brannan/portolani/pull/208#discussion_r4331707913), [docs/voyages](voyages/README.md))
