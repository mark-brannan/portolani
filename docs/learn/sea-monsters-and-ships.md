# Sea monsters and ships

Sea monsters are beings from folklore, imagined at great size, often shown threatening ships or spouting water. Some may rest on real animals, such as whales and giant squid.

## On this map

The map's monsters and ships are cut from the Carta Marina of 1539, by Olaus Magnus. The sea of that map is filled with many animals, whales and walrus among them. They are cut with the water round them, so a beast and a ship sit in the sea instead of on the parchment. See [charts and makers](charts-and-makers.md) for the map itself.
